Bridging Data, Systems, and Human Understanding

Profile · Updated regularly

I am an early-career researcher focused on making complex data systems more accessible, interpretable, and efficient. My work sits at the intersection of databases, machine learning, and visualization, with the long-term goal of translating theoretical advances into tools that impact real-world decision making.

I am currently exploring how interactive systems and model efficiency can reduce the barrier between humans and large-scale data, particularly for users outside traditional computer science domains.

Research Focus

My research investigates lightweight machine learning models, database optimization, and human-in-the-loop systems. I am especially interested in questions of scalability, interpretability, and responsible deployment of AI systems.

Recent Writing & Work

As a researcher just starting out, I focus on building strong foundations through workshop papers, technical reports, and open-source prototypes. Formal publications will be listed here as they appear.

Teaching

I have served as a teaching assistant for courses in introductory programming, data structures, and database systems. I am passionate about making technical concepts accessible to students from diverse backgrounds.
